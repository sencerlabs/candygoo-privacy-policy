## English — Privacy Policy

This policy explains how CandyGoo, offered under the GooGames brand, handles information in its offline Android version.

### Information stored on your device

CandyGoo stores game progress, scores, lives, boosters, Goo Crystals and language preferences in the app's local storage. Local technical records, diagnostic logs and technical identifiers may also be retained to operate the game. The app does not provide an online account or automatic account synchronization.

### Network access, advertising and payments

This offline version does not request Android's INTERNET permission. It does not provide automatic transmission of your game saves or usage analytics to GooGames servers. Advertising and real-money in-app purchases are not offered in this version. Goo Crystals are a local virtual game currency. Libraries inherited from the game engine may remain in the package; we do not claim that the app contains no third-party code.

### Backups you choose to create

If you choose Create backup, your game records are written to a file you select. That file may contain progress, inventory, virtual currency and technical identifiers. The backup is not offered as an encrypted vault. Keep it in a safe location. If you select a cloud or third-party document provider in Android's file picker, you initiate that transfer and that provider's own terms apply. CandyGoo does not automatically upload the backup. Automatic Android cloud backup and automatic device-to-device transfer of the app's data are excluded in this version.

### Retention and deletion

Local app data remains until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. These actions can erase your progress. Exported backup files are separate and are not automatically deleted when the app is uninstalled; delete them from the location where you saved them. Restoring a backup replaces your current local save. No server-based save recovery is provided.

### Support communications

If you voluntarily email support@sencer.app, your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send are handled to answer your request. Do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. You may contact the same address about your support correspondence or request its deletion, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

### Contact and policy changes

For CandyGoo support or privacy questions, contact support@sencer.app. If the app's information-handling practices change, this policy and the relevant store disclosures will be updated. The app store, operating system and any document provider you choose operate under their own privacy policies.
